# Case file C08: Village outskirts along a former defensive berm

> Invented case for an academic exercise. The country context comes from the public sources listed in the repository's README. The area, its ID, the people (named by role only), the survey and the national criteria box are invented, and do not describe a real hazardous area or a real national standard.

| Field | Entry |

## 2. How the area was recorded
The area was recorded in 2019 from a police report of devices removed in front of the berm that the occupiers built around the village. Families returned in 2018 and have not used the land in front of the berm since.

## 4. Community meeting
Held at the mukhtar's guest house on 16 February 2026. The men (10) and the women (8) met separately. A police officer from the local station brought the 2019 record. Herders said they keep their flocks on the other side of the village.

## 6. Survey team remarks
A located removal of devices and a located accident show improvised mines along the berm. The team recommends clearance along the berm, with technical survey to set the extent in front of it.
