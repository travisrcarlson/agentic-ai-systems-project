# Case file C09: Mountain terraces below a former outpost

> Invented case for an academic exercise. The country context comes from the public sources listed in the repository's README. The area, its ID, the people (named by role only), the survey and the national criteria box are invented, and do not describe a real hazardous area or a real national standard.

## 2. How the area was recorded
The 2024 village-by-village survey recorded the area on the elders' report that the outpost had been protected with mines. The upper terraces have not been farmed since the 1990s, although the land is good.

## 4. Community meeting
Held at the mosque guest room on 11 May 2026 with the men (15) and the elders. The female surveyor met the women (12) separately in a house in the village. Everyone keeps away from the painted stones; children are told not to cross them.

## 6. Survey team remarks
There is direct evidence on the terraces and on the path, and the informal removal of mines from the path does not count as clearance. The team recommends clearance of the terraces and the path.
