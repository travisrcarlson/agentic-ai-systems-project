# Case file C01: Hillside meadows below a former front line

> Invented case for an academic exercise. The country context comes from the public sources listed in the repository's README. The area, its ID, the people (named by role only), the survey and the national criteria box are invented, and do not describe a real hazardous area or a real national standard.

## 2. How the area was recorded
The area was recorded in 1998, during an early survey that drew suspected areas along both sides of the former front line from wartime maps and interviews. The record holds no item, accident or marking in the area. The front line ran along the ridge above the meadows; its eastern end is a separate ridge, outside this area.

## 4. Community meeting
Held in the village hall on 13 May 2026. The men (12 farmers and herders) and the women (8) met separately; the female community liaison officer led the women's meeting. The landowner and a former soldier who served on the ridge attended the men's meeting and were then interviewed alone. Both meetings said that nobody has been hurt on the meadows since the war, and that the only accident people remember happened on the eastern ridge before it was cleared.

## 6. Survey team remarks
The suspicion rests only on the line drawn in 1998 along the former front. The minefield that the unit laid is on the eastern ridge, and it was recorded, cleared and certified in 2009. Both meadows have been used for many years. The team recommends cancelling the whole area.
