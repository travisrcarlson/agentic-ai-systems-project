# Case file C07: A road and river crossing by a former military position

> Invented case for an academic exercise. The country context comes from the public sources listed in the repository's README. The area, its ID, the people (named by role only), the survey and the national criteria box are invented, and do not describe a real hazardous area or a real national standard.

## 1. Area record

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Area ID | AGO-SHA-4408 |
| Country | Angola |
| Setting | A road and river crossing by a former military position in the south-east. Mines were laid around bases, supply routes and crossings during the wars, often unmarked and unrecorded. |
| Recorded status | Suspected hazardous area (SHA), from the national survey in 2019 |
| Suspected hazard | Anti-personnel and anti-vehicle mines |
| Parts | A: the road approach and the crossing; B: the riverbank on either side of the crossing |
| Survey | Non-technical survey by an accredited survey team, 6–8 April 2026 |
| Field visit | Completed |
| Community meeting | Held |
| Groups consulted | Men; women (in a separate meeting); two former combatants; the traditional authority; a herder |

## 2. How the area was recorded
The national survey recorded the area in 2019 on reports from returnees that the crossing had been defended. The road has been closed to traffic since then. The provincial roads office plans to rebuild the crossing.

## 4. Community meeting
Held at the traditional authority's compound on 7 April 2026. The men (13) and the women (10) met separately. The two former combatants live in the village and were interviewed alone. Everyone avoids the crossing, and the women use the upstream footpath to reach the water.

## 5. Land use
Years of safe use count the whole years of use with no finds and no accidents.

## 6. Survey team remarks
The warning signs, the fence remains, a located animal accident and a detonation in a bush fire confirm mines at the crossing. The team recommends clearance of the road approach and the riverbank before the road works start.
