The area, its ID, the people (named by role only), the survey and the national criteria box are invented, and do not describe a real hazardous area or a real national standard.

## 2. How the area was recorded
The national database holds a record of a "dangerous area" by the old garrison road. Neither its source nor the date it was entered is known. The team tried to visit in August 2026 but turned back at a flooded river crossing, and filled in the survey form from a phone call with a local leader.
