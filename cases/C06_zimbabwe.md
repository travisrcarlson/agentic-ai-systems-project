# Case file C06: The far margin of a border minefield's hazardous area

> Invented case for an academic exercise. The country context comes from the public sources listed in the repository's README. The area, its ID, the people (named by role only), the survey and the national criteria box are invented, and do not describe a real hazardous area or a real national standard.

## 2. How the area was recorded
The 2016 re-survey drew the CHA as a wide band along the border minefield and divided it into sectors. Sectors 2 and 3, next to sector 4, were cleared in 2023–2025. Sector 4 is not yet cleared. Its far margin, away from the minefield line, is the area reviewed here; it has been farmed for years.

## 4. Community meeting
Held under the meeting tree on 20 August 2026. The men (11) and the women (9) met separately; the village head attended the men's meeting. People know where the old fence line runs and keep away from it. They said the margin has always been worked.

## 5. Land use
Years of safe use count the whole years of use with no finds and no accidents.

## 6. Survey team remarks
The margin has been farmed for years, and it lies beyond the belt in which mines were found in the neighbouring sectors. The team recommends cancelling the margin.
