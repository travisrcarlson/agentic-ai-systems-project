# Case file C14: Border farmland, a legacy minefield and a strip near a recent clash site

> Invented case for an academic exercise. The country context comes from the public sources listed in the repository's README. The area, its ID, the people (named by role only), the survey and the national criteria box are invented, and do not describe a real hazardous area or a real national standard.

| Field | Entry |

| Country | Thailand, on the border with Cambodia |
| Setting | Farmland and scrub in a border district, below a ridge along a stretch of the border that is not demarcated. ## 2. How the area was recorded
An impact survey in 2001 recorded the area as suspected, from villagers' reports of a minefield at the foot of the ridge. In 2019 technical survey confirmed mines there, defined the edge of the minefield and marked it; the farmland lies outside that edge. After armed clashes near the ridge road in 2025, the military closed the road, and the survey team could not enter the strip along it.

## 4. Community meeting
Held at the village temple on 11 August 2026. The men (12) and the women (10) met separately; the female team member led the women's meeting. The village head and the farmers who work part A took part. Everyone knows where the marked minefield is and keeps out of it. Since the 2025 clashes, nobody goes up to the ridge road.

## 6. Survey team remarks
The farmland has been used safely for more than twenty years and lies outside the minefield that technical survey defined in 2019. The minefield at the foot of the ridge is confirmed. The team could not enter the strip along the ridge road. Given the reports of new mines laid by the neighbouring country's forces, the team recommends clearance of the whole area, including the strip, as soon as the military allows.
