# Case file C03: Border farmland with a strip along the forest edge

> Invented case for an academic exercise. The country context comes from the public sources listed in the repository's README. The area, its ID, the people (named by role only), the survey and the national criteria box are invented, and do not describe a real hazardous area or a real national standard.

## 2. How the area was recorded
The 2012 baseline survey recorded the area because it lies near former positions on the border and people avoided it. No mines or accidents were recorded in the area itself. Since then, families have ploughed most of it. The strip along the forest edge has never been farmed and is covered in dense scrub.

## 4. Community meeting
Held at the pagoda on 9 June 2026. The men (14) and the women (10) met separately. Farmers from the fields and the commune chief took part. Both meetings agreed that the fields are safe to farm. Nobody could say what is in the strip; people keep out of it because of what they have heard.

## 5. Land use
Years of safe use count the whole years of use with no finds and no accidents.

| Part | Use | Since | Years of safe use |
|---|---|---|---|

## 6. Survey team remarks
Part A has been ploughed for ten years without incident. Part B has never been used, and the only report about it is second-hand. No direct evidence was found anywhere in the area, so the team recommends cancelling the whole area.
