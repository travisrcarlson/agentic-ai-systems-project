# Case file C11: A wadi crossing east of the berm

> Invented case for an academic exercise. The country context comes from the public sources listed in the repository's README. The area, its ID, the people (named by role only), the survey and the national criteria box are invented, and do not describe a real hazardous area or a real national standard.

## 1. Area record

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Area ID | ESH-SHA-0921 |
| Country | Western Sahara, east of the berm |
| Setting | A seasonal wadi crossing in the southern sector, east of the berm and outside the buffer strip. A recorded minefield lies upstream, nearer the berm. Nomadic herders use a water point on the terrace in years with rain, and a track crosses the wadi bed. |
| Recorded status | Suspected hazardous area (SHA), recorded in 2024 after an EOD spot task found a mine on the wadi bank |

| Parts | A: the high terrace by the water point; B: the wadi bed and its flood deposits, where the track crosses; C: the north-eastern edge, next to a restricted area |

| Field visit | Completed, except part C |
| Parts not reached | C: the team was not allowed near it under restricted-area instructions |
| Community meeting | Held |
| Groups consulted | Herder families at the water point: men; women (separately, with the female team member); a driver who uses the track |

## 2. How the area was recorded
The area was recorded in 2024, after an EOD team destroyed a mine found on the wadi bank following a flood. The survey team drew the area to cover the terrace, and the wadi bed and its flood deposits downstream of the recorded minefield. The north-eastern edge was added "pending access", because the team could not go near it.

## 4. Community meeting
Held at the water point on 10 March 2026, with the herder families camped there. The men (6) and the women (5) met separately; the female team member led the women's meeting. A driver who uses the track joined the men's meeting. Families move with the rains, so some who use the terrace in other years were not there.

## 6. Survey team remarks
The terrace has been used safely for many years and lies above the flood line. The mine found in 2024 was carried by a flood from upstream, so more may lie along the wadi bed. The team could not go near the north-eastern edge. The team recommends technical survey of the whole area, including the north-eastern edge once access is agreed.
