# Case file C02: Resettled gardens and paddy near a former army position

> Invented case for an academic exercise. The country context comes from the public sources listed in the repository's README. The area, its ID, the people (named by role only), the survey and the national criteria box are invented, and do not describe a real hazardous area or a real national standard.

## 2. How the area was recorded
In 2010 an army engineer report listed a "dangerous area" near the former position, with an approximate location and no boundary. It was entered in the national database as an SHA. In 2014 the army minefield beside the position was cleared and handed over under its own minefield record. In March 2026 the national database unit compared the two records in a desk review.

## 4. Community meeting
Held at the village temple hall on 4 March 2026, with separate meetings for the men (9) and for the women's rural development society (11). Returnee households and the village officer attended. Everyone agreed that the land has been worked since the families returned, and that nothing has been found or reported.

## 5. Land use

## 6. Survey team remarks
The 2010 record duplicates the army minefield beside the position, which was cleared and handed over in 2014. The land has been in daily use since 2017. The team recommends removing the duplicate record from the database and cancelling the area.
