# Case file C05: Leased fields beside a forest belt in a de-occupied village

> Invented case for an academic exercise. The country context comes from the public sources listed in the repository's README. The area, its ID, the people (named by role only), the survey and the national criteria box are invented, and do not describe a real hazardous area or a real national standard.

## 1. Area record

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Area ID | UKR-SHA-5526 |
| Country | Ukraine |
| Setting | Leased fields beside a forest belt at a village in the east, occupied in 2022 and de-occupied later that year. Mixed contamination is reported in the region. |

| Groups consulted | Men; women (in a separate meeting); the village administrator; the leaseholder |

## 2. How the area was recorded
The area was recorded in 2023 from a list of settlements where troops had been present during the occupation. No items, accidents or positions in the area were recorded at the time. Few residents stayed in the village during the occupation.

## 3. Evidence log

| ID | Evidence | Source | Location | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | The 2023 record lists the area as suspected because troops were present in the village in 2022; no items, accidents or positions in the area were recorded. | National database, 2023 settlement list | Whole area | 2023 |
| E2 | "These fields must be mined; the soldiers were everywhere around the village." He was not in the village during the occupation and repeats what residents told him. | Village administrator | Whole area | Interview, 3 September 2026 |

| E5 | Two women who stayed in the village during the occupation said that soldiers dug in along the forest belt but did not go onto the open fields, which were in view of the village. | Two women residents who stayed during the occupation | Parts A and B | Women's meeting, 3 September 2026 |
| E6 | The team walked the field tracks and saw no items, signs or fencing. It did not enter the forest belt. | Survey team observation | Part A; the edge of Part B | 3 September 2026 |

## 4. Community meeting
Held at the village administration on 3 September 2026. The men (7) and the women (9) met separately; most were people who returned after the occupation. The village administrator attended the men's meeting. The two women who stayed during the occupation spoke at the women's meeting and were then interviewed together.

## 5. Land use
Years of safe use count the whole years of use with no finds and no accidents.

| Part | Use | Since | Years of safe use |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Wheat, harvested by machinery | 2025 | 1 |
| B | Not used; forest belt and its edge | — | 0 |

## 6. Survey team remarks
The administrator and the women who stayed in the village disagree about the open fields. The leaseholder has used the fields for one season. To be safe, the team recommends clearance of the whole area.
